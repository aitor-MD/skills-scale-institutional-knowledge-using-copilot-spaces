## OctoAcme project management process summary

OctoAcme follows an iterative, customer-focused project lifecycle. Work begins with initiation: validating the business need, defining stakeholders, and creating a lightweight one-pager with measurable outcomes and early risks. After the initiative is approved, planning turns the idea into an actionable backlog with priorities, estimates, dependencies, milestones, and a clear Definition of Done.

During execution, the team uses project boards, standups, pull request reviews, CI, and testing to deliver small, validated increments. Risks and blockers are tracked and escalated through a defined path so issues are addressed before they create delivery or customer impact. Releases require acceptance criteria, passing checks, rollback planning, smoke testing, and stakeholder communication to ensure a reliable deployment process.

After each sprint, release, milestone, or incident, the team runs a retrospective and records action items with owners and deadlines. This creates a continuous improvement loop that helps the organization adjust processes, strengthen collaboration, and deliver better outcomes over time.

The documentation also defines key project roles, responsibilities, and artifacts used by the team to coordinate effort, communicate status, and maintain alignment.
